For my master's research, I studied acoustic normal modes in thin accretion disks around rotating black holes under the supervision of Prof. Pankaj Jain and Prof. J. S. Yadav.

![Conceptual visualization of wave modes in a rotating accretion disk](/assets/img/project_concepts/accretion-disk-concept.png){: .img-fluid .rounded .z-depth-1 }

_Conceptual visualization of coherent wave modes in a rotating accretion disk._

Using an effective Kerr potential, I derived a dispersion relation valid across a range of black-hole spin parameters. The project connected analytical modelling with questions in high-energy astrophysics.
