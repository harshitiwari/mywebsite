During my FARE fellowship at IIT Kanpur, I worked on high-resolution simulations of dry and moist turbulent convection in Earth's troposphere.

![Cloud-bearing atmospheric convection viewed over a wide landscape](/assets/img/projects/moist-convection.jpg){: .img-fluid .rounded .z-depth-1 }

_Atmospheric convection couples buoyant turbulence, moisture, and large-scale circulation._

The project studies how moisture, conditional instability, and intermittency alter turbulent transport. It connects fundamental convection physics with processes relevant to atmospheric flows and monsoon dynamics.

**Research questions**

- How does latent heating reorganize turbulent transport?
- What controls intermittency in conditionally unstable convection?
- How do dry and moist systems differ across spatial scales?
