At New York University, I work with Prof. Katepalli R. Sreenivasan on quantum computing for fluid dynamics (QCFD). The project explores how quantum algorithms can represent and solve challenging problems in fluid mechanics.

![Conceptual visualization of quantum circuits merging into a turbulent flow](/assets/img/project_concepts/qcfd-concept.png){: .img-fluid .rounded .z-depth-1 }

_Conceptual visualization of quantum representations meeting multiscale fluid motion._

The work brings together turbulence, numerical analysis, and quantum computation, with the goal of identifying fluid-dynamics problems where quantum methods can offer a meaningful advantage.

**Current focus**

- Quantum formulations of fluid-dynamics problems
- Numerical benchmarks against classical solvers
- Algorithms that remain physically interpretable and computationally practical
