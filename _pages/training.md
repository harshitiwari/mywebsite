<section class="training-section training-cycle-section">
  <div class="training-section-heading">
    <p>5-WEEK WAVE</p>
    <h2>Build. Load. Peak. Recover.</h2>
  </div>
  <p class="training-section-intro">The current heavy week ends with the 130 kg deadlift. Week 0 starts Monday, September 21 as the deload, then the cycle repeats through Weeks 1–4. Accessories stay familiar enough to measure progress, but can be exchanged when recovery, equipment, or weak points demand it.</p>
  <div class="training-cycle-grid training-cycle-grid-five">
    <article><span>01</span><small>RPE 6</small><strong>Technique</strong><p>Top single, then 3 × 5 at ~70%. Fast, repeatable reps with about four reps still available.</p></article>
    <article><span>02</span><small>RPE 7</small><strong>Build</strong><p>Top single, then 3 × 5 at ~72.5%. Add load while preserving the same depth, pause, and bar path.</p></article>
    <article><span>03</span><small>RPE 8</small><strong>Strength</strong><p>Top single, then 3 × 5 at ~75%. Heavier and specific, but never a grinder.</p></article>
    <article class="is-peak"><span>04</span><small>RPE 9–10</small><strong>Peak</strong><p>Test one controlled single, then 3 × 5 at ~70% if form is stable. Accessories stay light.</p></article>
    <article class="is-deload"><span>00</span><small>RPE 5</small><strong>Deload</strong><p>3 × 5 at 50–55%. Restore movement quality, absorb the block, and make space for the 5 km benchmark.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="training-peak-ladder">
    <div>
      <p>WEEK 4 · COMPOUND LIFT</p>
      <h3>A deliberate path to one rep.</h3>
      <span>Take longer rests as the weight rises. Use safeties or a spotter, and stop the ladder if technique changes.</span>
    </div>
    <ol>
      <li><b>10 reps</b><span>Empty bar / lightest full-height deadlift setup</span></li>
      <li><b>5 reps</b><span>~50% of current 1RM</span></li>
      <li><b>3 reps</b><span>~65%</span></li>
      <li><b>1 rep</b><span>~75%</span></li>
      <li><b>1 rep</b><span>85–90%</span></li>
      <li><b>1 rep</b><span>92–97% · confidence single</span></li>
      <li class="is-pr"><b>1 rep</b><span>PR attempt · only with stable form</span></li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="training-section training-strength-sessions">
  <div class="training-section-heading">
    <p>STRENGTH DAYS</p>
    <h2>The exact work after the main lift.</h2>
  </div>
  <p class="training-section-intro">Every compound lift begins with the same progressive warm-up: light × 10, 30% × 5, 40% × 3, then three ascending singles toward the day’s prescribed RPE. After the day’s top single, perform three back-off sets of five at roughly 70–80% of that single. The accessories stay stable enough to track.</p>
  <div class="training-strength-grid">
    <article>
      <span>MONDAY · LEGS</span><h3>ATG squat</h3>
      <ul><li>Bulgarian split squat or dumbbell lunge · 2 × 10 / leg</li><li>Leg extension · quads · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Leg curl · hamstrings · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Hip abductor + adductor or weighted glute bridge · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Calf raise · 3 × 10–12</li></ul>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>TUESDAY · PUSH</span><h3>Bench press</h3>
      <ul><li>Incline dumbbell bench · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Seated pec fly machine · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Triceps pushdown · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Seated dumbbell overhead triceps extension · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Dumbbell pullover · 3 × 10–12</li></ul>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>WEDNESDAY · SHOULDERS</span><h3>Overhead press</h3>
      <ul><li>Seated dumbbell press · 16 kg / hand baseline · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Lateral raise · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Cable upright row · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Seated rear-delt fly machine · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Reverse cable curl, hammer curl, wrist curl · 3 × 10–12 each</li></ul>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>FRIDAY · PULL</span><h3>Deadlift</h3>
      <ul><li>Lat pulldown · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Seated cable row or machine row · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Dumbbell row · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Weighted back extension / hyperextension · 3 × 10–12</li><li>Shrugs · 3 × 10–12 if recovery is good</li></ul>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
