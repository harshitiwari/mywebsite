<section class="training-section training-cycle-section">
  <div class="training-section-heading">
    <p>5-WEEK WAVE</p>
    <h2>Build. Load. Peak. Recover.</h2>
  </div>
  <p class="training-section-intro">Each compound lift follows four progressive weeks, then one deload week. Accessories stay familiar enough to measure progress, but can be exchanged when recovery, equipment, or weak points demand it.</p>
  <div class="training-cycle-grid training-cycle-grid-five">
    <article><span>01</span><small>RPE 6</small><strong>Technique</strong><p>3 × 5 on the main lift. Fast, repeatable reps with about four reps still available.</p></article>
    <article><span>02</span><small>RPE 7</small><strong>Build</strong><p>4 × 5. Add a small amount of load while preserving the same depth, pause, and bar path.</p></article>
    <article><span>03</span><small>RPE 8</small><strong>Strength</strong><p>4 × 3. Heavier and specific, but never a grinder. Finish knowing one or two good reps remained.</p></article>
    <article class="is-peak"><span>04</span><small>RPE 9–10</small><strong>Peak</strong><p>Test one controlled single on squat, bench, overhead press, and deadlift. Accessories drop sharply.</p></article>
    <article class="is-deload"><span>05</span><small>RPE 5</small><strong>Deload</strong><p>2 × 5 at 50–55%. Restore movement quality, absorb the block, and make space for the 5 km benchmark.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="training-peak-ladder">
    <div>
      <p>WEEK 4 · COMPOUND LIFT</p>
      <h3>A deliberate path to one rep.</h3>
      <span>Take longer rests as the weight rises. Use safeties or a spotter, and stop the ladder if technique changes.</span>
    </div>
    <ol>
      <li><b>10 reps</b><span>Empty bar / lightest full-height deadlift setup</span></li>
      <li><b>5 reps</b><span>~50% of current 1RM</span></li>
      <li><b>3 reps</b><span>~65%</span></li>
      <li><b>1 rep</b><span>~75%</span></li>
      <li><b>1 rep</b><span>85–90%</span></li>
      <li><b>1 rep</b><span>92–97% · confidence single</span></li>
      <li class="is-pr"><b>1 rep</b><span>PR attempt · only with stable form</span></li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>

    <h2>Faster without stealing strength.</h2>
