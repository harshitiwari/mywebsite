## A life outside simulations

Research is a large part of my life, but not the whole of it. I am happiest when curiosity becomes physical: a long route through the Himalayas, a hard training session, a run that clears the mind, or a piece of music that changes the texture of a quiet evening.

## Mountains, teams, and perspective

Adventure sports taught me a useful complement to computational research: prepare carefully, communicate clearly, and stay calm when the plan meets reality. The same habits matter whether a team is moving through the mountains or debugging a large simulation.
